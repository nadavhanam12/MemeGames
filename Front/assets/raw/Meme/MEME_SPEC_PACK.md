# Meme Spec Pack — Hormuz Hold'em, Batch 1

Pre-written meme concepts for the art chat. Each spec below is a **complete
creative decision**: the format (based on a proven viral meme), the joke
tension, the four cell scenarios, the cast, and the caption zones are all
locked here. The art chat's job is **execution only** — do not invent new
scenarios, swap casts, or change compositions.

Rules of use:

- Read together with `INSTRUCTIONS.md` (pipeline rules) and `GAME_BRIEF.md`
  (game context, cast likenesses, approved style). Those files govern
  everything not specified here.
- Generate **one spec at a time**, on request ("generate spec #3"), as one
  2048×2048 atlas per spec.
- Cell order is always top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right.
- Formats marked **[nested]** have internal panels: reproduce the complete
  internal panel structure inside every 1024×1024 quadrant.
- All caption zones stay completely blank (runtime text only).
- Copyrighted/real-person source characters are already substituted in the
  specs; keep the substitute but preserve the source composition exactly.
- **Cast note:** the main-cast rule in `GAME_BRIEF.md` (Trump and/or Mojtaba
  in every quadrant) overrides the per-cell casts written below — adapt the
  cast while keeping each cell's scenario category and joke tension.

### Spec 1 — Expanding Brain **[nested]**
*Based on: Expanding Brain / Galaxy Brain*

- **File:** `meme_expandingbrain_atlas.png`
- **Format lock:** 4 stacked horizontal panels per quadrant. Right side of
  each panel: the same character's head in profile, brain progressively
  glowing — normal head → lit brain → beaming brain → cosmic glowing head
  with light rays. Left side of each panel: blank caption zone.
- **Core tension:** escalating self-declared genius vs. increasingly absurd
  ideas.
- **Caption zones:** 4 blank left half-panels per quadrant.
- **Cells:**
  - TL (victory): trader head — escalating oil-market galaxy-brain plays.
  - TR (failure): tanker captain head — escalating "shortcuts" through the strait.
  - BL (antagonist): Iranian commander head — escalating attack schemes.
  - BR (reaction): news anchor head — escalating hot takes.

### Spec 2 — The Plan Backfires **[nested]**
*Based on: Gru's Plan*

- **File:** `meme_grusplan_atlas.png`
- **Format lock:** 4 internal panels (2×2) per quadrant. A presenter stands
  beside a large blank flip-board, gesturing at it. Panels 1–2: confident
  presenting pose. Panel 3: presenter glances at the board, smile gone.
  Panel 4: identical to panel 3 (the double-take repeat is mandatory).
- **Core tension:** confident three-step plan whose third step is a disaster
  the presenter only notices mid-presentation.
- **Caption zones:** the blank flip-board in each internal panel.
- **Cells:**
  - TL (victory-gone-wrong): tanker captain presenting an escort plan.
  - TR (failure): oil executive presenting a profit plan.
  - BL (antagonist): Mojtaba Khamenei presenting a blockade plan.
  - BR (reaction): Trump presenting a "peace through winning" plan.

## Test protocol (batch 1 pilot)

1. Paste `INSTRUCTIONS.md`, `GAME_BRIEF.md`, and this file into the art chat.
2. Ask for **one spec only** (start with Spec 4 — single panel, no nesting,
   easiest to validate; then Spec 2 — hardest, nested with a mandatory
   repeated panel).
3. QA against `INSTRUCTIONS.md` §11 plus, for spec-pack atlases: every cell
   matches its spec's scenario and cast exactly — a beautiful cell with the
   wrong cast or scenario is a rejection.
4. Note any spec ambiguity the generator stumbled on; report it back so the
   spec (not the chat improvisation) gets fixed.
